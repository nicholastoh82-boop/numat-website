The Story

Demand is the missing link in nature investment.

Most platforms in this space fund supply: identity, traceability, finance, carbon. Useful, necessary, and not enough.

NUMAT was founded in September 2024 on a simple conviction: none of it pays the farmer until somebody actually buys the bamboo. So we built the buyer.

Eleven months later, in August 2025, the factory in Manolo Fortich came online. Today: paying customers, four continents in the pipeline, growing every month. Backed by Wavemaker Impact.

Why Now

Three reasons this matters at scale.

01

We are the offtaker.

Smallholder farmers across Mindanao have a buyer with published prices and verified offtake volumes. Income is real, not promised.

02

Carbon that pays.

Bamboo regrows in three to five years versus twenty five for hardwood. Sequestration plus revenue, not sequestration alone.

03

Built for export.

Specifications, lead times, packaging, and supply continuity engineered for international project work. Not a craft brand.
